Madelina Brixton serves a range of vegan-labelled baguettes, bagels, and a Mediterranean vegan brunch plate among its mostly non-vegan menu. The cozy cafe is also known for its vegan banana bread, doughnuts, and smoothies made with dairy-free milks. It has a small indoor space complemented by a terrace, and occasional wine bar events add a social dimension in the evenings.
