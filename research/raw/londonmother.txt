Slow Coffee in a Busy Spot: Brixton’s Best Cafés
Slow London
Brixton isn’t just busy markets and loud energy, it’s also home to some of the best independent coffee shops in the city.
If you know where to look, you’ll find quiet corners, rich coffee, and space to pause in the middle of it all.
Here are 13 spots worth visiting when you want to slow down in South London.
Even if it’s just for a moment.
Balance
179 Ferndale Rd, London SW9 8BA
Step into Balance, the luxurious cafe that opened in 2017, and experience all it has to offer.
From creamy smoothies to scrumptious vegetarian and vegan dishes, the menu offers something for every coffee and food enthusiast.
Enjoy the best drinks seven days a week and indulge in a truly unique and localized experience.
Come and see why Balance is the destination spot for all your caffeine cravings and gastronomic delights.
Blank Coffee Brixton
144 Acre Lane, Brixton, London SW2 5UT
Indulge in the exquisite environment of Blank Coffee and treat yourself to an extraordinary brunch.
An exclusive fusion of British, Jamaican, and New Zealand flavours awaits you, presented in a lavish and hospitable atmosphere.
An outing here will undoubtedly be one of the ultimate cafe experiences you can have.
Brixton Blend
8 Tunstall Road, Brixton, London, SW9 8BN
Grab yourself a coffee at Brixton Blend, a speciality shop situated in the heart of Brixton.
Here, you can unwind and savour a cup of locally-crafted coffee, paired with gourmet meals that will tantalize your taste buds.
Whether you’re seeking an elegant retreat or simply wish to delight in the exquisite flavours of locally brewed coffee, Brixton Blend is an essential stop.
Cafe Van Gogh
88 Brixton Road, London SW9 6BE
Enjoy a luxurious coffee experience at Cafe Van Gogh – an exquisite vegan cafe featuring seasonal ingredients, frequently changing menus every 6-8 weeks and a not-for-profit social enterprise.
Drink a delectable cup of coffee knowing that you’re helping the community with your purchase.
Caya
344 Coldharbour Lane, London, SW9 8QH
If you’re searching for a luxurious workspace to take your work to the next level, look no further than Caya.
With its dynamic and vibrant atmosphere, Caya offers a collaborative and creative environment that is second to none – not to mention the exquisite speciality coffee available to enjoy as you work remotely.
F. Mondays
436 Coldharbour Lane, London, SW9 8FN
In 2014, Ken and Ciara had a grand ambition to construct a gathering place for the local neighbourhood.
With the assistance of their allies, they endeavoured to make their vision a reality.
The end result?
An enchanting spot where friends could socialize over a hot cup of coffee.
As news of this exquisite shop spread, it evolved to include an abundance of products from local businesses, plus a Food Truck right in the centre of Brixton.
What began as a dream has now become a highly cherished part of the area.
Federation Coffee
Brixton Village Cafe, Unit 77-78, Coldharbour Lane, London, SW9 8PS
Unlock the hidden depths of flavour in coffee at Federation Coffee.
Savour the intricate seasonal aromas and flavours of the newest blends from Curve Roasters.
Or, elevate your coffee experience with one of Federation’s signature baked goods, such as their decadent espresso-soaked banana bread.
Visit this luxurious cafe and enjoy the unique selection of coffees and treats.
Madelina Brixton
105 Acre Lane, Brixton Hill, London SW2 5TU
Experience the best of brunch at Madelina, the trendiest cafe in town.
Pamper yourself with a classic breakfast, delectable vegan bagel sandwiches, and gluten-free treats finished off with a delicious cup of coffee.
Enjoy the luxurious atmosphere and stunning fare of Madelina while you live the brunch life.
Parissi
76 Atlantic Road, London, SW9 8PX
From a young age, Spyros Parissis was already cultivating an affinity for the culinary arts.
As he grew, he was gifted with a wealth of recipes by his mother and grandmother, and it was with this great skill that he decided to share his love of baking with the world.
This dream was realized when Parissi, a sophisticated coffee shop and bakery, was born.
Allowing guests to indulge in the finest coffee and pastries, Parissi is a destination for the most discerning palate.
Pipoca Vegan
224a Brixton Road, London, SW9 6AH
Indulge in the luxurious ambience of Pipoca Vegan, a zero-waste vegan cafe and store.
Treat your taste buds to a decadent plant-based crepe, or take home some freshly-ground coffee beans and grains – however you choose, you can be sure that it’s helping to protect the environment.
Step inside and revel in all that Pipoca Vegan has to offer.
Ritzy Cinema Cafe
Ritzy Picturehouse, Brixton Oval, Coldharbour Lane, London, SW2 1JG
Step into a world of grandeur and savour the ambience of the Ritzy, an iconic cinema that has been around for generations.
Part of the Picturehouse Cinemas family, the Ritzy is an exquisite destination for cinephiles and gastronomes alike.
Come and luxuriate in the exquisite cafe and bar and stay to marvel at the myriad of magnificent films.
Indulge in a cup of coffee and a sumptuous bite as you take in a classic movie masterpiece.
San Marino Brixton
413 Brixton Road, London, SW9 7DG
Embark on an exquisite Italian getaway without leaving London at San Marino Brixton.
This luxurious family-run Sicilian coffee shop, established in 1993, is the ultimate destination for exquisite paninis and freshly brewed coffee.
With the capacity to host up to 140 guests, San Marino has expanded since its inception in a small location that only could accommodate 25.
Indulge in the refined atmosphere of a family-run Italian coffee shop and savour the exquisite flavours of Italy.
As well as Italian cuisine, they also offer a luxurious English breakfast with a selection of vegetarian and vegan options.
Stir Coffee Brixton
111 Brixton Hill, London, SW2 1AA
Indulge in the sophisticated atmosphere of Stir Coffee, where “Good Vibes Matter”!
Relax and unwind in the inviting space, while being warmly welcomed by the attentive staff.
If you’re seeking something unique, be it a steaming cup of coffee, a delectable pastry, or a craft beer like no other, you’ll find your perfect pick at Stir Coffee.
Brixton is a paradise for coffee connoisseurs, boasting a plethora of exquisite coffee shops.
For those in search of the perfect cup of joe, we present this curated list of the finest coffeehouses in the district.
From classic coffeehouses to unique cafe-grocery combos, you are sure to find something to suit your sipping needs.
Whether you’re looking to indulge in a leisurely cup or grab a quick takeaway, Brixton has something for everyone.
With so many delectable options, it’s no wonder why the coffee scene in Brixton is thriving.
