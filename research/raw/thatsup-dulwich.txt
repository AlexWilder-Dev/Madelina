Visit breezy café Madelina for brunch in Dulwich. The interior is homey and plenty of outdoor seating is also available. The menu is simple but covers all the essentials, including lots of options for veggie and vegan guests. Choose from cooked breakfasts, salads, or sandwiches and wraps stuffed with tempting combos like chicken, pesto, and cheese or roasted vegetables and hummus.
