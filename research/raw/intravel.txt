About
Madelina Dulwich, located in London, is the perfect spot for those seeking a cozy and casual dining experience without breaking the bank. With an impressive Google rating of 4.7, this eatery offers a blend of delightful options suited for breakfast, brunch, and lunch, all at an affordable price per person ranging from £10 to £20.
Guests can enjoy a range of specialties, including the renowned full English breakfast, delicious smoothies, and the best coffee in town. The menu caters to various dietary preferences, offering vegan, vegetarian, and gluten-free options alongside healthy and fresh salads.
The cheerful staff and fantastic sandwich selections create a warm atmosphere that is ideal for a family dinner, a meet-up with friends, or even a solo dining experience. You can choose to dine-in, take your meal to-go, or enjoy it on the outdoor terrace with complimentary Wi-Fi.
With an array of small plates, delightful desserts, and quick service, Madelina Dulwich ensures a satisfying meal for everyone. Whether you're grabbing a quick coffee or savoring a leisurely brunch, this charming spot in London is sure to leave you with a lovely experience.
