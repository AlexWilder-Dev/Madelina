Madelina Brixton
A proper neighbourhood café on Brixton Hill doing excellent coffee and seasonal brunch in a genuinely welcoming space.
Closed
4.4(182)105 Acre Ln, Brixton Hill, London SW2 5TU, UK
🍴 cafecosycasualrelaxedwelcomingcafe
Head up Acre Lane towards Brixton Hill and you'll find Madelina tucked away in a stretch that's quietly become rather good for eating and drinking. It's the kind of place where locals actually hang about—not because it's fashionable, but because the coffee's genuinely well-made and the food actually tastes like someone's thought about it. The room itself feels effortlessly put-together: natural light, a bit of greenery, the sort of unfussy vibe that makes you want to linger over a second espresso. Whether you're grabbing breakfast before work or settling in with a paper on a Saturday morning, there's no pretence here, just solid hospitality.
