Madelina
madelinalondon
Madelina operates charming cafe spaces in Brixton and Dulwich, serving specialty coffee, seasonal brunch dishes, and refreshing smoothies daily
Madelina is a dual-location destination in London offering a laid-back environment for specialty coffee, fresh pastries, and hearty brunch plates. Visitors can enjoy signature menu items like avocado toast, veggie breakfast plates, and loaded breakfast bagels. The Dulwich site features a scenic garden terrace perfect for alfresco dining throughout the summer months. In addition to daily service, the team hosts social wine nights and accommodates private hire requests, bringing a welcoming community focus to both the Brixton and East Dulwich neighborhoods.
Frequently Asked Questions
What kind of food and drink is served at Madelina?
Madelina serves specialty coffee, brunch dishes, smoothies, pastries, and wine.
Where are the Madelina locations in London?
Madelina has two locations: one in Brixton and one at 75 East Dulwich Grove in East Dulwich.
Do I need to book a table at Madelina?
While walk-ins are welcome, you should send a direct message to book a table for wine nights or for visiting the Dulwich garden.
Does Madelina offer vegetarian options?
Yes, the menu includes vegetarian-friendly options such as avocado toast and a signature veggie breakfast.
